The current editorial covers use oversized typography and three staggered iPhones, based on the owner’s visual references and existing Rewind screenshots. OpenAI image generation created the composition from those references; screen details are illustrative rather than pixel-exact. No additional stock-photo scene was introduced. An alternate vector-only cover remains available. Earlier illustrated cover artwork is archived. It is a campaign illustration; the film demonstrates the product using real screenshots and recordings. Brand assets identify Rewind and are not a grant of trademark rights. No font binaries or standalone third-party device render are distributed here.
